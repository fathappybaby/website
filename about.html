House Standards

Every movie requires:

  1. A dog takes a dump on camera. Rules are rules.
  2. A child gets possessed. Not always by what you'd expect.
  3. A limb gets ripped off. We count the head as a limb.
  4. Green screen. We are incredible with green screen.
  5. A table.
  6. Gray sheets for a backdrop. We have a sheet steamer on standby at all times.

Secret Weapon

Behind every FHB short is Sean Dog, our writer, kept firmly off-camera and off IMDb. He's our greatest strength and our most invisible one.
